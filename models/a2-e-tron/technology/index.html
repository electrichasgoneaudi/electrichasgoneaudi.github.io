Audi A2 e-tron

Audi A2 e-tron technology

Driver assistance, sensors, infotainment and connectivity in the Audi A2 e-tron, including trained parking, reverse assist and the AI-backed Audi assistant.

Last updated Sep 8, 2026
Audi A2 e-tron Variants Exterior Specifications Interior Drivetrain Technology

Audi fits the A2 e-tron with a driver assistance and infotainment package that is unusually complete for an entry-level model, and much of it is standard rather than optional.

Standard driver assistance

Every A2 e-tron leaves the factory with:

  • Emergency brake assist with forward collision warning
  • Swerve assist — supports an evasive steering maneuver once the driver initiates one
  • Attention assist / drowsiness alert
  • Adaptive cruise control
  • Park assist plus with a reversing camera
  • Cross-traffic alert and rear cross-traffic alert
  • Traffic sign recognition
  • Efficiency assist — uses navigation and traffic data to prompt lifting off early
  • Automatic emergency steering
  • ABS, electronic stability control and traction control

Optional driver assistance

  • Adaptive cruise assist plus with emergency assist, which brings the car to a controlled stop if the driver becomes unresponsive
  • Front cross-traffic assist
  • Lane keeping and lane departure warning
  • Surround-view cameras with a 3D model of the car and its surroundings
  • Park assist plus with remote control from a smartphone
  • Trained parking — the car learns and repeats up to five parking maneuvers
  • Reverse assist — memorizes roughly the last 50 meters driven below 35 km/h and retraces it
  • Proactive occupant protection

Night vision is not available on this model.

The optional corner radars and additional cameras are what enable the surround-view system, front cross-traffic assist and the more advanced parking functions — which is why those options tend to be bundled in the Tech packages rather than sold individually.

Infotainment and connectivity

The MMI panoramic display combines an 11.9-inch Audi virtual cockpit plus with a 12.8-inch curved MMI touch display. An augmented-reality head-up display is optional.

Key software features:

  • Audi assistant with integrated AI, handling natural-language requests beyond fixed voice commands
  • e-tron route planner with predictive charge scheduling, which preconditions the battery and plans stops around the actual charging curve
  • Audi connected work — Microsoft Outlook and Teams integration for calendar and meetings
  • Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, standard
  • In-car navigation, standard
  • Voice control, standard
  • Wireless phone charging with Qi 2.2 at up to 25 W

Gesture control is not offered.

Technology packages

The optional equipment is grouped into three tiers:

  • Tech — the entry bundle
  • Tech plus — adds the more advanced assistance and lighting options
  • Tech pro — the fullest specification, including the surround-view system and the advanced parking functions

Alongside these sits the efficiency package, which is not a technology bundle as such but the combination of sport suspension and aerodynamic measures that unlocks the model’s lowest consumption figures.

Charging and energy management

Beyond the route planner, the A2 e-tron manages energy through:

  • Four driving modes — comfort, balanced, efficiency and dynamic
  • Four recuperation levels, from D1 to D3 on the paddles plus a B position for one-pedal driving
  • Battery preconditioning ahead of a planned DC charging stop, triggered by the route planner
  • Bidirectional charging via Vehicle-to-Load, and Vehicle-to-Home in Germany, Austria and Switzerland
