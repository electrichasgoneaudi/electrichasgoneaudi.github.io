## Reading the range figures

Audi publishes two WLTP numbers for each version. The higher one applies to the most efficient specification — smaller wheels, the leanest equipment and, where offered, the efficiency package. The lower one applies to the top trim with larger wheels and more equipment. The gap is significant: on the 125 kW version it is 47 km, and on the 170 kW version it is 66 km.

Audi's marketing figure of **12.8 kWh/100 km** for the 140 kW car is the consumption **including charging losses**. Measured the conventional WLTP way, without charging losses, the same car uses **11.3 kWh/100 km**. Both numbers are correct — they just measure different things, and the distinction matters when comparing the A2 e-tron against rivals that quote only the tailpipe-equivalent figure.

## Equipment lines

Two exterior lines are offered across the range:

- **Base line** — a restrained treatment with body-colored bumpers and lighter detailing
- **S line** — more strongly sculpted bumpers, black design elements and a sportier overall stance

On top of that come the **Tech**, **Tech plus** and **Tech pro** packages, which bundle the assistance, lighting and infotainment options, and the **efficiency package**, which combines a sport suspension with the aerodynamic measures that unlock the lowest consumption figures.
